# Brand Voice

## Brand voice summary

Maureen Ella should sound like an experienced bridal beauty artist who is warm, calm, organized, and emotionally intelligent. The voice should feel premium but not cold, personal but not casual, feminine but not overly cute, and expert without sounding intimidating.

## Voice pillars

### Calm confidence

Use language that makes brides feel safe, prepared, and guided.

Examples:

- Your wedding morning should feel calm, beautiful, and on schedule.
- We plan the beauty timeline with care so you can be present for the moments that matter.
- From your preview to your final touch-up, every detail is handled with intention.

### Editorial romance

Use soft visual language around beauty.

Examples:

- Soft, romantic hair.
- Glowing, photo-ready makeup.
- Polished, timeless bridal beauty.
- Effortless elegance.

### Founder-led expertise

Maureen is the trust anchor. Use founder perspective when appropriate.

Examples:

- Maureen brings artistry, timeline awareness, and a calming presence to every wedding morning.
- After years of bridal mornings, Maureen knows the small details that help the day flow.

### Education and preparedness

The site should teach before selling.

Examples:

- Before your preview, we will help you prepare your hair, skin, inspiration photos, and accessories.
- Our prep guides are designed to help you arrive confident and ready.

## Words to avoid

- cheap
- slay
- glam squad as primary positioning
- hurry up
- flawless guarantee
- miracle
- cure
- perfect skin promises
- book now everywhere
- best ever unless quoting a review

- Bridal beauty that feels as calm as it looks beautiful.
- Wedding morning beauty, planned with care.
- Romantic bridal hair and makeup for brides who want to feel calm, confident, and beautifully prepared.
- A premium bridal beauty experience rooted in artistry, organization, and confidence.
