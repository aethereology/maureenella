# Social Content Plan

## Content pillars

### Bridal proof

- Bride before/after, with permission.
- Final look closeups.
- Bridal party moments.
- Reviews.
- Timeline wins.

### Bridal education

- Hair prep tips.
- Makeup prep tips.
- Trial prep tips.
- Extensions guidance.
- Veil/accessory tips.
- Parking/logistics tips.

### Founder trust

- Maureen behind the scenes.
- Salon suite.
- Wedding morning kit.
- Personal story.
- What Maureen has learned from weddings.

### Real weddings

- Venue/location reels.
- Vendor shoutouts.
- Bride story.
- Look breakdown.

### Education for artists

- Inquiry tips.
- Timeline tips.
- Pricing boundaries.
- Client experience tips.
- CRM/workflow tips.

### Favorites

- Lip colors.
- Fragrance.
- Hair prep products.
- Extensions.
- Business tools.

## Weekly cadence

- 1 portfolio/review post.
- 1 educational carousel.
- 1 behind-the-scenes story/reel.
- 1 blog/guide promotion.
- 1 vendor/location tag post when available.

## Instagram carousel ideas

- 7 Things to Bring to Your Bridal Preview.

## Pinterest strategy

Create pins for:

- Prep guide checklists.
- Real wedding case studies.
- Bridal hair looks.
- Curly bridal hair guide.
- Hair extension guide.
- Lip color guide.
- Veil/accessory guide.

Pinterest is especially useful for bridal inspiration and affiliate/product content.
