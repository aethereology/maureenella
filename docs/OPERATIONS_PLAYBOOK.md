# Operations Playbook

## Bridal client workflow

### 1. Inquiry

Collect:

- Bride name
- Email
- Phone
- Wedding date
- Venue
- Getting-ready location
- City/state
- Desired ready-by time
- Hair service count
- Makeup service count
- Bride services needed
- Trial/previews desired
- Extensions/veil/accessory notes
- Planner/photographer if known
- Inspiration/vision
- How they found Maureen

### 2. Qualification

Check:

- Date available?
- Service minimum met?
- Location/travel feasible?
- Timeline feasible?
- Additional artist needed?
- Bride matches brand/service expectations?

### 3. Quote and next steps

Send:

- Availability confirmation
- Service estimate
- Date reservation requirements
- Contract process
- Trial/prep guide links
- Timeline expectations

### 4. Contract and date reservation

- Signed agreement.
- Deposit/date reservation.
- Payment records.
- Service counts and location.

### 5. Bridal preview/trial

Before preview:

- Send trial prep guide.
- Request inspo photos.
- Request dress/neckline info.
- Request accessories/veil if available.
- Ask about allergies/sensitivities.

After preview:

- Record products and look notes.
- Save photos.
- Note adjustments.
- Confirm wedding-day plan.

### 6. Timeline planning

Inputs:

- Ready-by time from photographer/planner.
- First look/ceremony/photo times.
- Service counts.
- Artist count.
- Travel/parking/setup time.
- Touch-up plan.

### 7. Final confirmation

Before wedding:

- Confirm services.
- Confirm location and parking.
- Confirm final invoice.
- Confirm timeline.
- Send prep guide.
- Assign contact person.

### 8. Wedding day

- Arrive on time.
- Setup.
- Execute timeline.
- Touch-ups within service window.
- Document content if permission granted.
- Pack out and leave area clean.

### 9. Post-wedding

- Ask for review.
- Ask for gallery/vendor credits.
- Request permission for portfolio/blog use.
- Tag vendors and couple as appropriate.
- Add to portfolio/case study pipeline.

## Content operations

### Monthly content cadence

- 1 real wedding or portfolio feature.
- 1 bridal prep/education post.
- 1 product/favorites post if affiliate active.
- 1 education/business post if mentorship active.
- Weekly Instagram repurposing from blog/portfolio.

### Content QA checklist

- Correct names.
- Correct venue/location.
- Photographer credit.
- Internal links.
- CTA.
- SEO title/meta.
- Alt text.
- Schema.
- Affiliate disclosure if needed.

## Review generation workflow

After wedding:

1. Send thank-you email.
2. Include Google review link.
3. Ask for permission to use review excerpt.
4. Ask for photo/gallery permission.
5. Add review to testimonial database.

## Education operations

Start with waitlist.

Then validate:

- What problems artists ask about.
- Which templates they want.
- How much Maureen wants to coach.
- Whether group coaching is viable.

Do not launch a large program before collecting interest.
