## Services cards

### Bridal Hair

Soft updos, romantic waves, polished buns, braids, curls, and veil-ready styles created around your dress, accessories, hair texture, and wedding-day vision.

### Bridal Makeup

Glowing, photo-ready makeup designed to feel like you at your most confident. Bridal makeup may include airbrush and lashes depending on your selected service and confirmed policy.

### Bridal Preview

A dedicated appointment to explore your hair and/or makeup look, test inspiration, discuss your dress and accessories, and create a plan for wedding morning.

### Bridal Party + Guests

Hair and makeup services for bridesmaids, mothers, family members, and guests who want to feel polished and ready for photos.

### Add-On Artist

## Process section

### 1. Inquire for your date

Share your wedding date, venue, getting-ready location, service count, and beauty vision.

### 2. Secure your wedding date

Once availability is confirmed, your signed agreement and date reservation hold your date.

### 3. Plan your preview

Your bridal preview gives us time to refine your hair and makeup vision before the wedding day.

### 4. Confirm your timeline

We use your photographer/planner timeline, service count, and location details to plan a smooth beauty schedule.

### 5. Enjoy the morning

On the wedding day, the focus is simple: calm energy, beautiful artistry, and everyone ready on time.

## About page starter

Maureen Ella is the artist behind Maureen Ella Bridal, a bridal hair and makeup experience based in St. Johns County, Florida. Known for romantic styling, glowing makeup, and calm wedding-morning energy, Maureen helps brides feel confident from the first preview to the final touch-up.

Her work is rooted in connection. Every bride brings a different story, style, and vision to the chair. Maureen's role is to listen, guide, and create a look that feels beautiful, personal, and lasting.

As a business owner, wife, mom, bridal artist, and mentor, Maureen brings both artistry and organization to her work. Her goal is not only to make brides look beautiful, but to help the entire beauty experience feel relaxed, thoughtful, and easy.
