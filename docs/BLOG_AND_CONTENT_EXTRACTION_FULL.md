## theparlor.info blog extraction

### Lash Extensions vs False Lashes

- Lash extensions are semi-permanent fibers applied individually by a licensed professional.
- Extensions last 4-6 weeks depending on growth and maintenance.
- Fills every 2-3 weeks.
- Oil-based products and rubbing eyes can reduce longevity.
- False lashes are temporary strips or clusters for single-day use.
- Extensions are higher commitment and customization.
- False lashes are lower cost and good for one event.
- Rebuild angle: Lash Extensions vs False Lashes for Your Wedding Day.

### Parking for Hair and Makeup Vendor

- Vendors bring tools, kits, lighting, and sometimes chairs.
- Parking delays can delay beauty services.
- Convenient parking helps unloading and setup.
- Brides should communicate with venue/hotel/Airbnb/home contact.
- Provide address, fees, restrictions, reserved spots, backup parking, permits, codes, and day-before confirmation.
- Assign a bridal party/family contact for guidance.
- Rebuild angle: Wedding Morning Logistics Checklist.

## Uploaded prep sheets

### Hair trial prep

- Submit front and back headshot.
- Wash hair day of or night before.
- Shampoo only in newer graphics.
- Blow-dry upside down for volume.
- Arrive with makeup on if not doing makeup trial.
- Wear white blouse/dress with similar neckline.
- Wear similar jewelry.
- Bring veil/accessories.
- Save 2-3 hair inspo photos.
- Bring preferred hair product.

### Makeup trial prep

- Submit front and back headshot.
- Arrive makeup-free.
- Wash, exfoliate, tone, moisturize.
- Groom eyebrows.
- Remove unwanted facial hair.
- Style hair similarly if not doing hair trial.
- Wear white/similar neckline.
- Wear similar jewelry.
- Save 2-3 makeup inspo photos.
- Bring favorite face/eye/lip product.

### Wedding-day hair and makeup prep

- Save inspiration photos.
- Wash hair day of or night before, shampoo only.
- Blow-dry upside down.
- Wear robe or button-down.
- Bring preferred product.
- Bring accessories.
- Remove makeup completely.
- Wash, exfoliate, tone, moisturize.
- Groom brows.
- Remove unwanted facial hair.
