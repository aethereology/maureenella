# Brand Architecture

## Umbrella brand

**Maureen Ella**

The public-facing domain is **maureenella.com**.

## Brand promise

Maureen Ella helps brides and beauty professionals step into their next chapter with confidence, artistry, and systems that make the experience feel calm and elevated.

## Brand pillars

1. **Confidence** - every service and resource helps someone feel more prepared.
2. **Calm execution** - wedding mornings and business operations should feel organized, not chaotic.
3. **Editorial beauty** - soft, polished, timeless, romantic visuals.
4. **Education** - clients are guided before the appointment; professionals are guided in business.
5. **Local expertise** - rooted in St. Augustine and Northeast Florida.

## Vertical map

### Maureen Ella Bridal

Audience: brides, bridal parties, destination brides, elopement brides.

Primary CTA: Request Availability.

Content: services, portfolio, reviews, FAQ, prep guides, real weddings.

### Maureen Ella Education

Audience: bridal hair and makeup artists, salon suite owners, beauty entrepreneurs.

Primary CTA: Join the Waitlist / Book a Strategy Call.

Content: mentorship, workshops, templates, business blog.

### Maureen Ella Journal

Audience: both brides and beauty pros, separated by category.

Primary CTA: Read, save, inquire, join list.

Content types: prep guides, real weddings, beauty tips, business advice, product guides.

### Maureen Ella Favorites

Audience: brides and beauty pros looking for curated products/tools.

Primary CTA: Shop Favorites / Read Guide.

Content: affiliate recommendations with editorial context.

## Naming conventions

Use:

- Maureen Ella Bridal, not The Parlor Bridal.
- Maureen Ella Journal, not Blog.
- Maureen Ella Favorites, not Shop, unless actual ecommerce launches.
- Request Availability, not Book Now, for wedding inquiries.

## Legacy handling

Old pages from theparlor.info and Wix should be redirected to new Maureen Ella pages. Do not delete SEO value without redirect mapping.
