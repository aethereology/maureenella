# Marketing Funnels

## Bridal inquiry funnel

### Traffic sources

- Google Search
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram
- Pinterest
- Vendor referrals
- Venue referrals
- Zola/WeddingWire/The Knot if active
- Blog SEO

### Entry points

- Home page
- Bridal page
- Portfolio
- Real wedding post
- Prep guide
- Location page

### Conversion path

1. Visitor sees local relevance and aesthetic proof.
2. Visitor reads reviews/portfolio.
3. Visitor checks services/FAQ.
4. Visitor clicks Request Availability.
5. Form captures complete details.
6. Maureen follows up with availability and next steps.

### Lead magnets

- Wedding Morning Hair Prep Checklist
- Wedding Morning Makeup Prep Checklist
- Bridal Trial Prep Checklist
- Curly Bride Prep Guide

## Education funnel

### Traffic sources

- Instagram for artists
- Pinterest business posts
- SEO business articles
- Existing beauty professional network

### Entry points

- Education landing page
- Instagram/Pinterest blog posts
- HoneyBook/Wix/tool posts

### Lead magnets

- Bridal Beauty Business Starter Checklist
- Inquiry Workflow Template
- Wedding Morning Timeline Template
- Instagram Bio Audit Checklist

### Nurture sequence

1. Welcome and Maureen story.
2. Biggest mistakes bridal artists make with inquiries.
3. How to organize a wedding morning timeline.
4. Pricing confidence and service minimums.
5. Case study / personal story.
6. Invite to workshop or strategy call.

## Affiliate funnel

### Traffic sources

- Prep guide SEO
- Product roundup SEO
- Pinterest pins
- Instagram stories
- Email list

### Conversion principles

- Recommend products in context.
- Use honest pros/cons.
- Add affiliate disclosure.
- Keep product pages editorial, not spammy.
- Track clicks.
