# Maureen Ella Education - Coaching Program Plan

## Positioning

Business mentorship for bridal hair and makeup artists who want to book better-fit brides, create smoother client workflows, price with confidence, and deliver a more organized wedding-day experience.

## Audience

- Newer bridal hair/makeup artists.
- Salon suite owners building bridal services.
- Beauty pros moving from occasional weddings to consistent bridal bookings.
- Artists who feel overwhelmed by inquiries, timelines, contracts, and social media.

## Problems to solve

- Unclear pricing.
- Weak inquiry process.
- No service minimum strategy.
- Chaotic wedding-day timelines.
- Poor Instagram/Pinterest positioning.
- No website or weak website.
- No content plan.
- Lack of confidence selling premium services.

## Signature framework

Possible framework: **The CONFIDENT Bridal Business Method**

- C - Clarify your bridal brand
- O - Organize your service menu and policies
- N - Nurture inquiries with better communication
- F - Fix your wedding-day workflow
- I - Improve portfolio and social proof
- D - Develop content and SEO systems
- E - Elevate client experience
- N - Nail your pricing and boundaries
- T - Track numbers and refine
