# Affiliate and Product Content Strategy

## Purpose

Use product content to support bridal prep, client education, and future affiliate revenue without weakening the premium service brand.

## Rules

- Product content must be helpful first.
- Always disclose affiliate relationships.
- Do not guarantee results.
- Do not make medical claims.
- Do not overwhelm bridal service pages with shopping links.
- Keep affiliate content mostly in Journal, Favorites, and prep guide sidebars.

## Recommended content model

Each product recommendation should include:

- Product name
- Category
- Why Maureen recommends it
- Best for
- Not ideal for
- When to use it
- Affiliate URL
- Disclosure
- Related guide
