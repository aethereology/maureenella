# Content Strategy Rules

- Keep bridal conversion primary in Phase 1.
- Education content belongs under `/education` or business-specific Journal categories.
- Affiliate content belongs under `/favorites` or Journal product categories.
- Do not let product recommendations interrupt wedding inquiry flows.
- Every blog post should have a reader goal, SEO goal, and conversion goal.
- Every real wedding post needs location, services, visual details, vendor credits, and CTA.
